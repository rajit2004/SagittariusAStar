# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, and healthy community.

Rhythma exists to make women's health information more accessible. This is meant to be a space where anyone, regardless of background, feels safe contributing code, content, translations, or ideas.

## Our Standards

**Behavior that contributes to a positive environment:**

- Being kind and respectful toward others
- Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences, including disagreements about technical decisions
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the women Rhythma serves
- Being patient with contributors who are new to open source or non-native English speakers
- Discussing menstrual and reproductive health topics in a mature, clinical, and respectful way, free of stigma

**Behavior that is unacceptable:**

- Sexualized language, imagery, or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting comments, or personal attacks
- Harassment, whether public or private
- Sharing others' private information without their permission
- Making light of or mocking menstrual or reproductive health topics
- Providing or soliciting unqualified medical advice within project spaces
- Any other conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies to all community spaces, including:

- GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, code review)
- Discord server
- Any official communication channel when representing the project

It also applies when you are officially representing the project in public spaces, such as using an official project email or posting via an official social media account.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and will explain moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by emailing:

**rhythma.official@gmail.com**

All reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially. Please include:

- Your contact information
- Names of individuals involved
- A description of what happened, with links or screenshots if relevant
- Whether the incident is ongoing

Do not report violations through public GitHub issues. Use the email above so the matter can be handled privately.

## Enforcement Actions

1. **Correction** - A private warning explaining the violation and why the behavior was inappropriate.
2. **Warning** - A warning with consequences for continued behavior. Temporary restriction from community interactions.
3. **Temporary Ban** - A temporary ban from community interactions for a specified period.
4. **Permanent Ban** - A permanent ban for repeated or severe violations.
